A quieter list.

Choose Work or Home. Type a task and press Enter. Paste several lines to add several tasks.

Tap a circle to cross a task off. Tap its words to edit. Tap its priority to cycle 1 → 2 → 3, with 3 the highest.

Move things: drag a task by its grip into a group, between tasks, or onto Work / Home. On a phone, drag the grip without holding. You can also change the list and group in the task editor.

Small steps: tap the little + beside a task to add a subtask. Press Enter to keep adding. Tap a bubble to cross it off; choose a small vertical list in Settings if you prefer.

Keyboard: N adds a task, / searches, Escape closes a panel. Focus a drag grip and press Space to open the task’s move options.

Advanced shorthand: Send drawings !3 #work ~30 @2026-12-04. Priorities also work in simple mode.
