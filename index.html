A SMALL GUIDE

Think on paper.

Write a task and press Enter. Keep typing to add the next. Paste several lines to add several tasks.

Tap a priority number to cycle 1 → 2 → 3. Three is highest. Tap a task’s words to edit it. Tap the circle to cross it off; tap again to bring it back.

On a touch screen, swipe a task right to finish it. You can always undo. In Advanced mode, add groups to keep related work together.

Quick shorthand (Advanced mode)
Send drawings !3 #work ~30 @2026-12-04
sets priority 3, tag work, 30 minutes and a due date.

Keyboard: N starts a new line, / searches, Escape closes a panel. Use Tab to reach every control.

Install in seconds.

On Android: open in Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install. On iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On desktop: use the install icon in your browser’s address bar.

Works offline after your first visit. No account, fonts, analytics or large downloads.
